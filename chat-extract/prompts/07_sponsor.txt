Detect sponsorship arrangement from a Russian Telegram message about a Schengen visa result.

"по спонсорству жены" = sponsored by wife → "spouse"
"спонсируемая/спонсируемый мной" = self is the sponsor → "self_is_sponsor"
"по спонсорству родителей" = sponsored by parents → "parents"
"по спонсорству" (generic, unclear who) = "unknown_sponsor"
If no sponsorship mentioned (applicant is financially independent) → null

Fields:
- has_sponsorship: true | false
- sponsor_relation: "spouse" | "parents" | "relatives" | "self_is_sponsor" | "unknown_sponsor" | null

Respond ONLY with valid JSON. No explanation.

{"has_sponsorship": true, "sponsor_relation": "spouse"}
